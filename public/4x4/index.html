Participant Information & Consent

You are invited to take part in a scientific study on the perception of sounds and images, conducted by Amit Gur and Asterios Zacharakis, University of Antwerp and Aristotle University of Thessaloniki. Please read the following before deciding to participate.

  • Voluntary participation. Taking part is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any moment by closing this window.
  • Use of data. The data obtained in this session will be used for a scientific study and may be published in anonymized, aggregated form in scientific journals and presentations.
  • Anonymity. No identifying information is collected. Your responses are stored under your Prolific identifier only, which is removed from the dataset before analysis and publication.
  • Risks. There are no known risks beyond those of ordinary computer use. Please set your volume to a comfortable level.
  • Ethical approval. This study has been approved by the Ethics Committee for the Social Sciences and Humanities of the University of Antwerp (reference SHW_2026_167, 22 September 2026).
  • Contact. Questions may be addressed to Amit.Gur@uantwerpen.be.
