Similarity Rating Task

Each round shows a reference — an image or a sound — with four items of the other kind.

Rate how similar each item is to the reference by moving its slider.

There are no right or wrong answers; we want your impression. Each sound must finish playing before its slider unlocks — replay as often as you like.
